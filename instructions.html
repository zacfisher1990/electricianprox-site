Getting started

Set up your account and run your first job in minutes

1
Create your account
Download Electrician Pro X from the App Store or Google Play, or open the web app at electricianprox.com. Tap Sign Up, enter your email and a password, then verify your email address.
2
Set up your business profile
Go to Settings → Business Profile and fill in your company name, logo, license number, address, and phone number. This info appears automatically on all estimates and invoices you send to clients.
Add your contractor's license number here — it prints on every document and builds client trust.
3
Connect Stripe to accept payments
Go to Settings → Payment Setup and tap Connect Stripe. Follow the short onboarding to link your bank account. Once connected, clients can pay invoices online with a card — funds deposit directly to you with zero platform fees.
4
Add your first client and job
Tap the + button on the Clients screen to add a client, then create a Job under that client. From the job you can build an estimate, track time, attach photos, and send an invoice — all in one place.

Clients

Keep all your customer info organized and searchable

1
Add a client
From the Clients tab, tap the + button. Enter their name, phone number, email, and service address. Clients can be residential homeowners, property managers, or commercial accounts — the record type is the same.
2
View client history
Tap any client to see all their jobs, estimates, invoices, and notes in one place. Every document you've ever sent them is a tap away — useful when clients call with questions about past work.
3
Search and filter
Use the search bar at the top of the Clients screen to find anyone by name, address, or phone number. Your whole customer list is always one tap away, even offline.
Add a short note to a client record to remember important job site details — gate codes, dog in yard, billing contact name, etc.

Jobs

Track every job from first visit to final payment

1
Create a job
Open a client record and tap New Job. Give the job a name (e.g. "Panel Upgrade — 200A"), set the job address if it differs from the client's main address, and add any notes about the scope of work.
2
Track job status
Move a job through status stages — Pending, Scheduled, In Progress, Completed — to keep your pipeline organized. Your jobs dashboard shows everything at a glance so nothing falls through the cracks.
3
Log time on the job
Inside a job, tap Time Tracking to start a timer or manually enter hours. Time entries are saved to the job record and can be used to calculate labor costs when building an estimate or invoice.
4
Attach photos
Tap Photos within a job to capture or upload images — before and after shots, panel labeling, inspection photos, or anything else you want documented. Photos are stored securely and tied to the job record.
Before-and-after photos are powerful proof of your work. They protect you if a client disputes quality and make your estimates more credible on future jobs.

Estimates

Build, send, and get sign-off on professional estimates

1
Start an estimate from a job
Open a job and tap New Estimate. Add line items for materials and labor — each line includes a description, quantity, unit price, and automatically calculated total.
2
Use AI Estimate Assist
Tap the AI Assist button and describe the job in plain English — for example, "200 amp panel upgrade with new meter base, 10 circuits." The AI will generate a starting set of line items you can review and adjust. It saves significant time on pricing common electrical work.
AI Assist gives you a solid starting point — always review the generated items and adjust quantities and pricing to match your local market and actual materials.
3
Send the estimate
Tap Send Estimate to email a professional PDF directly to your client. The estimate includes your business name, logo, license number, line items, and total — everything a client needs to approve the work.
4
Convert to invoice
Once the client approves the estimate, tap Convert to Invoice. All line items carry over automatically — no re-entry needed. Then send the invoice and collect payment.

Built by an electrician

The estimate line items and AI Assist suggestions are based on real field experience. Descriptions, units, and default categories reflect how electricians actually write up work — not generic contractor templates.

Invoices & payments

Send professional invoices and get paid online

1
Create or convert an invoice
Create an invoice directly from a job, or convert an approved estimate. Add line items for labor and materials, apply any discounts, and set a due date.
2
Send the invoice
Tap Send Invoice to email your client a professional PDF with a secure payment link included. The email comes from your business email so it looks professional, not like generic software.
3
Client pays online
Your client taps the payment link and pays by card through Stripe. Funds are deposited directly into your linked bank account — EPX charges zero platform fees on top of standard Stripe processing rates.
Stripe's standard processing rate is 2.9% + 30¢ per card transaction. There are no additional fees from Electrician Pro X — what Stripe charges is all you pay.
4
Mark invoices paid
When a client pays by check or cash, open the invoice and tap Mark as Paid to keep your records accurate. Paid invoices are separated from outstanding ones on your dashboard so you always know what's owed.

NEC calculators

20+ code-based calculators — free for all users

All calculators are available to every user on every plan, including free accounts. No paywall, no limits — these tools belong on the job site.

Wire sizing
NEC Table 310 ampacity calculations for copper and aluminum conductors in any conduit fill and temperature rating.
Load calculations
Residential and commercial load calcs including optional and standard methods for service sizing.
Conduit fill
Calculate maximum wire fill for any conduit type and size per NEC Chapter 9 tables.
Voltage drop
Single-phase and three-phase voltage drop with automatic wire size recommendation to stay within 3% / 5%.
Ohm's Law
Solve for voltage, current, resistance, or power — enter any two values and get the rest instantly.
Box fill
Calculate minimum box size required for a given combination of conductors, devices, clamps, and fittings.
Motor calculations
FLA, breaker sizing, and wire sizing for single-phase and three-phase motors per NEC Article 430.
+ more calculators
Panel schedule helper, transformer sizing, grounding electrode conductor, fault current, and more — open the Calculators tab to see all 20+.
