Why Electricians Need Their Own Field Service App

Jobber, Housecall Pro, ServiceTitan were built for "home services." That means plumbers, landscapers, HVAC techs. Electrical contractors get shoehorned into a generic mold that was never designed for the way they actually work.

Electrician Pro X is a field service management app built exclusively for electrical contractors. Every client record, every job workflow, every estimate and invoice, designed around how electrical businesses actually operate, not adapted from something else.

And because it's built for electricians specifically, you get something no generic field service app offers: 30+ NEC calculators and code reference tools baked right in, ready whenever you need them on the job site. Your office software and your field tools in one app.
