1
Invite a crew member
Open the More menu and tap Crew (on the web app, it's in the left sidebar), then tap the + button (Invite on the web app). Enter your crew member's email and tap Send Invitation. They don't need to have EPX yet — if they haven't signed up, the invitation waits for them and appears once they create a free account with that email. Pending invitations are listed under Pending, where you can Cancel Invite.
2
They accept from their Inbox
Your crew member opens their Inbox in EPX and taps Accept. That's it — they're on your crew.
Crew members work for free. They don't need any subscription to join your crew, see assigned jobs, or work from their schedule — only the account inviting and assigning needs the Crew plan.
3
Assign and schedule jobs
On the Crew screen, tap a crew member to expand their card, then tap Assign Job. Pick the job and tap Next — Pick a Date. Choose one or more days (start and end times are optional) and confirm, or tap Skip to give them the job without a schedule. Every assigned job shows up in their Jobs list; scheduled days also appear on their Schedule calendar. To add days to a job you skipped, assign it again and pick dates. View Jobs and Time Cards on the same card show what they're working on and their hours; a job assigned without a schedule is listed as No dates set. You can also assign from the job itself: open the job and tap Assign Crew under Team Members to pick one or more crew members at once, then their dates.
4
Change or reassign a day
Tap View Jobs on a crew member's card, then the pencil next to a day. Choose Change date & time to pick a new day, adjust the times, or tap No Date, or choose Reassign to someone else to hand that day to another crew member. Everyone affected is notified, and when someone's last day on a job moves away, the job comes off their list. The trash button removes a day.
5
Follow progress from the field
On an assigned job, crew members tap On My Way (with the option to text the client), Start Job, and Finish Job. Start Job clocks them in and Finish Job clocks them out. You're notified of each update, and the job preview's Team Members shows where each person is, such as Started · 8:02 AM. The job moves to In Progress when someone starts and to Completed once everyone assigned has finished; you can always change the status yourself. If you'd rather complete jobs yourself (handy for multi-day jobs), tap Settings on the Crew screen (the gear on a phone) and turn off Complete when everyone finishes. You're still told when everyone has finished.
To make sure jobs are really done, set what every job needs in Settings on the Crew screen, under Before finishing, every job needs: a number of photos, every to-do checked off, and/or the customer's signature. To change it for one job, open the job, find Before Finishing and tap Edit (or Set up); Use my defaults for this job puts it back. The last person on the job can't tap Finish Job until those are in place; the app lists what's missing. Anyone who finishes earlier isn't held up. Tap Get customer signature to have the customer sign on the phone. The signature shows on the job, in the exported job report, and as Work completed and accepted on any invoice for that job.
6
Message the crew on a job
Open a job that has crew assigned and tap the Messages tab. Everything posted there is shared between you and everyone on that job, with the newest message at the top. Everyone else gets a notification for each message; tapping it opens the job right on its Messages tab, and a red dot on the tab means there's something new. In the Inbox, messages on the same job are grouped into one item (for example, "3 new messages on Panel swap"). Tap the camera button to send a photo, with a caption if you like; tap a photo to see it full size. New messages also show under Alerts in your Inbox; tap one to open the job's Messages tab.
Too many notifications from a busy job? Tap Mute at the top of its Messages tab. You'll still see the red dot, but no notifications for that job until you tap Muted to turn them back on. Only the people on the job can see its messages; if you take someone off the job, they can no longer see the thread. Messages can't be edited or deleted once sent.
7
Dispatch from the board
On the Crew screen, tap the Dispatch tab at the top (it appears once you have crew members). Each person gets a row, starting with You, and you can switch between Week and Day or jump back with Today. Jobs no one is on yet are listed under Unassigned: tap one, then tap a day in someone's row. You can also start from the day: in the Week view, tap a day in someone's row. It's highlighted, and the Unassigned list above the board becomes a list of jobs for that day; enter a start and end time if you like (optional), then tap a job to add it. Tap the day again or the ✕ to cancel. In the Day view, tap + Add a job on someone's card instead. With a start time, that person (you included, on your own row) also gets a reminder an hour before. On the web app you can also drag: drag a job from Unassigned onto someone's day, or drag a job already on the board to another day or another person to move or reassign it (they're notified). That list also has jobs that are already assigned, so you can add a second person to a job. If that day already has work, you'll see a warning and the button reads Add anyway, and days that are double-booked are outlined in red. Tap a job already on the board to change its date or time, reassign it, open it, or remove it from that day (the crew member is notified). On your own row, tapping a job lets you set its time, open it, or take it off that day. On a phone, the week shows each job as a colored bar (see the key under the board); tap a date at the top, or Open day, to see a day's jobs in full.
Colors show where each job stands: Scheduled, Clocked in, On the way, Started, and Done. To change a crew job's time later, tap it and choose Change date & time.
8
Review hours, approve, and export for payroll
Tap Time Cards on a crew member's card to see their hours by week. Tap the pencil on an entry to change the day, start, end or notes, or to delete it, and tap Add a missed entry if they forgot to clock in; they're notified of any change you make. Tap Set a pay rate to see each week's regular hours, overtime (1.5×), double time (2×, where your rule has it) and estimated pay before taxes. Choose how overtime is counted under Overtime in the Crew screen's Settings: Weekly over 40 (the default; federal rule and most states), Daily over 8, weekly over 40 (for example Alaska and Nevada), Colorado (past 12 in a day or 40 in a week) or California (past 8 in a day or 40 in a week, double time past 12 in a day, and special rules for a 7th straight workday). Only you can see pay rates. Inside a week, tap Approve week when the hours are right. An approved week is locked, so the crew member can't change those hours, and if anything changes afterwards it shows Changed since approval; tap Reopen to unlock it. To run payroll, tap Hours on the Crew screen and pick a period (this week, last week, last 2 weeks, this month or last month) to get a spreadsheet (CSV) of every crew member's entries, with regular, overtime and double-time hours, rate and estimated pay.
On the web app the spreadsheet downloads; on a phone, the share sheet opens so you can email it or save it to Files. It opens in Excel, Google Sheets or Numbers.
9
Remind your crew about their jobs
Tap Settings on the Crew screen (the gear on a phone) to choose when your crew hears about their day. Morning of sends each crew member one notification listing their jobs that day, with start times, the client and the address; pick a time from 5:00 to 9:00 AM. 1 hour before sends a heads-up before each job that has a start time. Turn on My morning summary to get one notification yourself each morning with who is working where. 1 hour before my own jobs (on by default) sends you the same heads-up for jobs on your own schedule that have a start time. Morning of and 1 hour before are on by default at 7:00 AM; changes save as soon as you tap them.
Reminders go out in each person's local time and only for days a job is scheduled on, not for jobs with no date or jobs marked Completed. Crew members need notifications turned on for the app, and they also show up in the Inbox. Tapping a reminder opens that job (a morning list with several jobs opens the Schedule), and tapping your morning summary opens Dispatch.
10
Add a manager
A manager, like an office manager or a partner, runs your crew with you from their own account. Under Managers at the bottom of the Crew screen, tap Invite a manager and enter their email. They get an email and an Inbox invitation; they need a free Electrician Pro X account to accept. Managers are free with your Crew plan. A manager can use the Dispatch board, see crew status, look at each person's jobs and time cards, fix and add time entries, approve weeks, and export hours. They can't invite or remove crew, change Crew Settings, or change your jobs, estimates or invoices, and they only see pay rates if you turn on Can see pay rates for them. Approvals by a manager show their name on the week. Remove a manager any time with the trash button.
If you're the manager: on the Crew screen, the Working in bar at the top switches between your own crew and each business you manage. Everything on the Crew screen and Dispatch then works on that business's crew. To stop, tap Step down.
11
Working for someone else?
The Working for section at the bottom of the Crew screen lists contractors you work for. Tap a contractor's card to see who's on that crew: the owner and your crewmates, by first name and photo. When a contractor assigns you a job, it shows up in your Jobs list, and on your Schedule for any days they scheduled you — no subscription required on your end. If they have reminders on, you get a notification the morning of with your jobs for the day and another about an hour before a job's start time. On the job, tap On My Way, Start Job and Finish Job to keep them posted (Start and Finish also clock you in and out). Use the job's Messages tab to talk with the contractor and the rest of the crew on that job. If the job lists items under Before Finishing, such as photos, to-dos or the customer's signature, take care of those before the last person taps Finish Job. When they approve a week of your hours, it shows Approved in Time Tracking and those hours are locked; ask them to reopen it if something needs fixing. Tap Leave Crew if you stop working with them.
