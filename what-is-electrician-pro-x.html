About the app

What is Electrician Pro X?

Electrician Pro X is an estimating, invoicing, and job management app with built-in NEC calculators for electricians and small electrical contractors. It runs on iOS, Android, and the web at app.electricianprox.com, and one account works on all three. It is made by Pro X Trades LLC, an Oregon company, and was built by a licensed electrician. The app includes 31 calculators and 27 reference tables based on the 2023 National Electrical Code (NEC). There is a free plan, and paid plans start at $9.99 per month.

Who it's for

Electrician Pro X is for:

  • Solo electricians who run their own service business and do their own quoting, billing, and code checks.
  • Small electrical contractors with 1–5 people. The owner can invite electricians, assign them jobs, schedule them, and see their time cards. Crew members use a free account.
  • Electricians doing side jobs who need a real estimate and invoice without paying for full business software.

It is built for these situations:

  • Quoting on-site. You build the estimate on your phone at the customer's house and send it by text or email before you leave. The customer can accept, sign, and pay a deposit from the link.
  • Sending an invoice from the truck. You turn the accepted estimate into an invoice and text the customer a payment link. They pay by card or bank transfer, and the invoice marks itself paid.
  • Sizing a circuit without the code book. You check ampacity with derating, voltage drop on a long run, or conduit fill against NEC 2023 tables in the app. The calculators work without cell signal on iOS and Android.

Who it's not for

Electrician Pro X does not have dispatch boards, GPS or vehicle tracking, multi-crew or multi-location scheduling, a call center, or marketing automation. Accounts have one owner and any number of crew members, with no dispatcher or office-staff roles. Larger shops that need those tools are better served by ServiceTitan, Jobber, or Housecall Pro.

It is also US-only. Calculations follow the 2023 NEC, not the Canadian Electrical Code, and the app is in English only.

What it does well

Estimates

You build estimates from line items, which you can mark as labor or material and group under section headings. Prices can come from your own saved materials list. You can add a discount, a required deposit, and contract terms. You send the estimate by text, email, or PDF, and the customer can accept it, decline it, or request changes from the link. Estimates with contract terms are signed with a typed-name e-signature. On paid plans, AI Estimate Assist suggests materials, labor hours, and a labor rate from a plain-language job description.

ExampleA service electrician quoting a 200 A panel upgrade describes the job in AI Estimate Assist, adjusts the suggested materials using their own saved prices, sets a 30% deposit, and texts the estimate from the driveway. The homeowner signs and pays the deposit by card from the link. The app then creates an invoice with the deposit already applied.

Invoices

You create invoices from scratch or from an estimate, with payment terms from Due on Receipt to Net 60. You send them by text, email, or PDF. On paid plans, customers pay online by card or ACH bank transfer through Stripe, and the money goes to your own Stripe account. You can also list Venmo, PayPal, Cash App, Zelle, or Square on the invoice, and record cash or check payments by hand. Invoices show as Draft, Sent, Partial, Paid, or Overdue, and you get an inbox alert when one goes overdue. Invoices, jobs, and expenses can sync to QuickBooks Online.

ExampleAfter a service call, the electrician converts the estimate to an invoice in the truck and texts the customer the link. The customer pays by card and the invoice changes to Paid before the electrician leaves the driveway.

Jobs

A job holds the client, location, notes, status (Scheduled, In Progress, Completed), a task checklist, and photos. You can mark up photos with a pen, arrows, text, and boxes. Each job tracks clock-in hours, expenses, mileage at the IRS rate, and its linked estimates and invoices. Jobs appear in a list or on a calendar, and the iOS and Android apps can add them to Google Calendar. On the Crew plan, you can assign jobs to crew members, who don't see the job's pricing.

ExampleOn a kitchen remodel rough-in, the electrician creates a job, marks up a photo of the panel to show where the new circuits land, logs the supply-house receipt as an expense, and records the drive. At closeout, the job shows total hours, expenses, and money collected.

NEC calculators

The app has 31 calculators and 27 NEC reference tables based on the 2023 NEC. Calculators that apply a code rule cite the NEC section, and every calculator reminds you to check your locally adopted edition and AHJ. They're free on every plan and work offline on iOS and Android.

ExampleBefore pulling wire for a 150-foot run to a detached garage, the electrician enters the load, voltage, and distance in the Voltage Drop calculator and gets the conductor size that keeps the drop under 3%.

Also included

Client records with call and text buttons, time cards (emailed or shared as a PDF), expense tracking with receipt photos and AI receipt scanning on paid plans, and a business dashboard showing revenue, profit, expenses by category, and overdue invoices, exportable to Excel.

What the NEC calculators cover

All 31 calculators are based on the 2023 NEC. Section numbers show the main code references each one uses.

Installation

  • Box Fill (314.16)
  • Pull Box Sizing (314.28)
  • Conduit Fill (Chapter 9, Tables 1, 4, 5)
  • Conduit Bending (offsets and saddles)
  • Cable Tray Fill (392.22)
  • Wire Pulling Tension
  • Torque Specs (110.14(D))
  • Underground Depth (Table 300.5)
  • Overhead Clearance (225.18, 225.19)
  • Working Space (110.26)
  • Support Spacing (300.11 and the conduit and cable articles)
  • Receptacle Spacing (210.52)
  • Lighting (Table 220.12)
  • Phase Color

Power calculations

  • Voltage Drop (Chapter 9, Table 8)
  • Ohm's Law (basic, series, parallel)
  • Reactance (inductive, capacitive, impedance, resonance)
  • Power Factor (capacitor kVAR sizing)
  • Power Triangle
  • Three-Phase
  • Short Circuit (available fault current; 110.9, 110.10)

Sizing and rating

  • Ampacity (Table 310.16 with temperature and bundling adjustments)
  • Load Calculations (Article 220, residential and commercial)
  • Service Entrance (Articles 220 and 230)
  • Grounding & Bonding (Tables 250.66 and 250.122)
  • Neutral Sizing (220.61)

Equipment

  • Motors (Article 430)
  • Transformers (450.3)
  • Solar PV (690.7, 690.8)
  • EV Charging (Article 625)

Utilities

  • Unit Conversion

Reference tables: 310.16, 310.17, 310.18, 310.15(B)(1), 310.15(C)(1), 210.24, 240.6(A), 250.66, 250.122, 314.16(A), 314.16(B), 430.7(B), 430.247, 430.248, 430.250, 430.52, Chapter 9 Tables 1, 4, 5, 8, and 9, Annex C, AFCI/GFCI requirements, a conduit bending reference, drill and tap charts (imperial and metric), and hole saw sizes.

Frequently asked questions

Is there an app that does voltage drop and invoices?

Yes. Electrician Pro X has a voltage drop calculator based on NEC 2023 in the same app as its estimates, invoices, and job tracking. It runs on iOS, Android, and the web.

How much does Electrician Pro X cost?

There's a free plan with 3 jobs, 3 estimates, and 3 invoices total. Solo is $9.99/month or $99.99/year, and Crew (for teams) is $12.99/month or $129.99/year. The NEC calculators are free on every plan.

Do I need an account?

Jobs, estimates, and invoices need a free account (email or Sign in with Apple). On the web, you can use the calculators at app.electricianprox.com/tools without signing in. The iOS and Android apps ask you to sign in first.

Does it work offline?

The calculators and NEC tables work fully offline on iOS and Android. Jobs, estimates, invoices, clients, and expenses you create or edit offline are saved on your phone and sync when you're back online. Sending documents, taking payments, and uploading photos need a connection.

Are the NEC calculators free?

Yes. All 31 calculators and 27 reference tables are included on every plan, including the free plan.

Which NEC edition does it use?

The 2023 NEC. Calculators that apply a code rule cite the NEC section, and every calculator reminds you to check your locally adopted edition and AHJ. The Canadian Electrical Code is not supported.

Can customers pay an invoice by card from their phone?

Yes, on the Solo and Crew plans. Customers pay by card or ACH bank transfer through Stripe, and the money goes into your own Stripe account. Stripe charges 2.9% + $0.30 per card payment and Electrician Pro X adds no fee.

Does it do dispatch or GPS tracking?

No. Electrician Pro X has no dispatch board, GPS, or fleet tracking. If you need those, look at ServiceTitan, Jobber, or Housecall Pro.

Do my crew members need their own subscription?

No. Crew members join with a free account and can see their assigned jobs and clock in. Only the owner who assigns jobs needs the Crew plan.

Does it connect to QuickBooks?

Yes. Invoices, jobs, and expenses can sync from Electrician Pro X to QuickBooks Online.
