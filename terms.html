Invoice Payments and Stripe

The App allows contractors to accept card and ACH bank payments from their clients. These payments are processed by Stripe through a Stripe account that belongs to the contractor (a "Connected Account"). Electrician Pro X provides the software that creates the payment page and records the result. We are not a party to the payment itself.

How Payments Work

  • To accept payments you must have an active Solo or Crew subscription and complete Stripe's onboarding to open a Connected Account. Stripe, not Electrician Pro X, decides whether to approve your account and which payment methods it may accept
  • When a client pays an invoice, the charge is made directly to your Connected Account. You are the merchant of record for that charge. Your business name, not ours, appears on your client's card or bank statement
  • Funds are paid out from your Connected Account to your bank account on Stripe's payout schedule. New Connected Accounts are subject to an initial payout delay of at least seven days while the account establishes a history
  • All payment processing is handled by Stripe and is subject to Stripe's Terms of Service and the Stripe Connected Account Agreement you accept during onboarding
  • We never see or store card numbers, bank account numbers, or other sensitive payment credentials. Your clients enter that information on a page hosted by Stripe
  • We are not responsible for payment processing errors, delays, declines, or failures, or for Stripe's decisions about your account

Processing Fees
